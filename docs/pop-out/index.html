Pop Out

A minimalist neon arcade game that will test your reflexes and your nerves. Spin and survive.

Get it on Google Play

How it plays

You only have one button, but you'll have to master the spin, the gravity and the growing chaos of bouncing balls.

Rotate the shape, avoid the red exits and clear every ball before time runs out. Each level brings new shapes, new challenges and new laws of gravity that turn everything upside down. Don't let the balls multiply.

Features

  • One-button controls: a single tap spins the whole shape.
  • A new shape on every level, from circles to pentagons and beyond.
  • Gravity that changes strength and direction as you play.
  • Power-ups that add extra time, weaken gravity, widen the exits or wipe out a whole colour.
  • Extra lives to earn when things get out of hand.
